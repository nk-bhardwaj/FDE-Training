We use Amazon’s customer return policy to determine if a purchased Fulfilment by Amazon item is eligible for return.

An automated system will reimburse the seller, if a unit has not been returned to the seller’s account within 45 days from the date the customer was refunded. This is also applicable for Partial refunds issued by the customer service department for orders with no information updated in the return report.

* The amount of the Referral Fee to be credited to your seller account will be calculated in accordance with the terms of your Selling Agreement.

This is a part of a 4-step workflow – below, you can see a summary of all steps and links to relevant help pages.

Customer feedback
Customers can leave feedback for sellers on orders that are fulfilled by Amazon just as they do for merchant fulfilled orders. When negative feedback is related to the fulfilment or services provided by Amazon that is not a Multi-Channel-Fulfilment, the negative rating can be struck through, so it will not affect your performance metrics. The customer’s comment will remain with a note from Amazon that states, “This item was fulfilled by Amazon and we take responsibility for this fulfilment experience”.

When a customer does not select Shipping or Customer Service as the reason for negative feedback, but the entire feedback is related to the service provided by Amazon, including fulfilment and customer service, for other than Multi-Channel-Fulfilment orders, you can request to have the negative rating struck through. We will review the feedback and if, in our judgement, it applies exclusively to the fulfilment or customer service provided by Amazon, we will strike through the negative rating and it will not reflect on your performance metrics. However, if in our judgement any portion of the comment applies to the condition of your product as indicated on your listing (New, Used etc.) or any service that you, rather than Amazon, provided or arranged to be provided to the customer, the feedback and negative rating will remain without edits. Use the Contact Us form to request a review of negative feedback.

Determining whether a returned unit is unsellable
A returned unit is considered unsellable when either of the following is true:

It is not in the same condition as when previously listed
It is defective, damaged, opened, lacking required labelling, is a Fulfilment by Amazon excluded product or is otherwise deemed unsuitable by Amazon, including units which may pose a health risk to our associates or the next customer who purchases it (this may include, but is not restricted to, consumables, personal care products and products with expiry dates).
Determining reimbursement eligibility
Reimbursement eligibility determinations for refunded units are usually made after inventory has been returned by the buyer to the Amazon fulfilment centre. This enables us to make the most accurate assessment of unit condition and reimbursement eligibility. Reimbursements for eligible units typically occur within 45 days after a customer refund is processed.

Providing Flexibility for Customers
When processing customer returns for units fulfilled through Fulfilment by Amazon, we apply Amazon.in ’s General Return Policy to determine whether a unit is eligible to be returned. The majority of the units returned to Amazon are received in sellable condition within 30 days of the original purchase in accordance with Amazon.in ’s General Return Policy. However, to ensure a great customer experience, which ultimately drives customer satisfaction and loyalty and makes Fulfilment by Amazon offers more attractive, we reserve the right to make case-by-case exceptions and accept returns for units fulfilled through Fulfilment by Amazon that may be outside these guidelines. While we extend this flexibility to customers, we routinely monitor for cases of abuse and take appropriate action. Please note that where we make an exception to Amazon.in ’s General Return Policy, you will still be required to reimburse us for any refund, adjustment or replacement made to the customer and pay to Amazon the Return Processing Fee for each customer-returned unit in the Clothing and Shoes categories unless the reason for the return is one for which Amazon takes responsibility.

In situations where we accept a return more than 30 days after the stated return window, we will reimburse you the replacement value for the returned item in accordance with the FBA Lost and Damaged Inventory Reimbursement Policy and the FBA Service Terms , unless we determine that the reason for the return is related to a recall, defect, violation of our policies or similar reason.

If we pay you the Replacement Value, the returned unit will not be added to your inventory and we will be entitled to dispose of the Unit pursuant to our FBA Service Terms.

All orders on Amazon.in that are fulfilled through FBA are subject to Amazon.in General Return Policy, the FBA Service Terms and Fulfilment by Amazon Programme Policies, and the terms of your Seller Agreement. Amazon’s decision to accept or decline a return and determination of the condition of the returned unit is final.

Issuing Refunds to Customers
Manually generated refunds remain in Pending status for up to two hours. You can cancel the refund by clicking “Cancel all pending refunds for this order”. If you cannot see this link, then the refund has been released to the buyer and cannot be cancelled.

To issue a refund, please follow these steps:

Locate the order you want to refund in Manage Orders. For more information about locating an order, go to Search Orders.
Click the Refund order button in the Action column.
Alternatively, click the Order ID and then click the Refund order button on the Order Details page.
Select a refund amount: full or partial.
To refund the full amount:

Select the Full Refund tab.
Required: Select the appropriate Reason for refund.
Optional: Add a Memo to buyer.
Optional: Add a Seller memo for your personal records. This allows you to track the actions you have taken on an order, including reasons for concessions and reminders of promises to buyers.
Click the Submit Refund button.
To refund a partial amount:

Select the Partial Refund tab.
Select the appropriate Reason for refund.
In the Amount to refund column, enter the refund amount. You can also refund gift wrap costs.
Optional: Add a Memo to buyer.
Optional: Add a Seller memo for your personal records.
Click the Submit Partial Refund button.
Allow up to 15 minutes for the data to be updated. Submitting the refund will automatically close any associated buyer return requests. You can contact the buyer from the Closed Orders section of your Manage seller-fulfilled returns page.
